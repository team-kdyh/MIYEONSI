# AFTER HOURS — project direction

Follow the active, validated Ouroboros Seed. These notes elaborate the user's delegated creative direction; they do not replace or reduce its acceptance criteria.

- The user asked for a chatbot-driven story game. Free text must work without first selecting a predefined intent, and what the player says must affect the scene and validated state. Logging custom text while routing only by a button index is insufficient.
- In live OpenAI mode, character speech must be genuinely composed in response to the player. Do not constrain the entire dialogue line to an enum of three canned replies, or use OpenAI only to choose a fixed phrase. Keep generated speech separate from validated semantic tags, canonical events, and server-owned transitions. Bound output length and validate the structured fields without eliminating the actual conversational experience.
- The romance must actually include getting to know an appealing adult character, mutual attraction, a confession, becoming a couple, and dates before the possessive thriller turn. Give Seo Yuna warmth, humor, preferences, awkwardness, and a distinctive voice. Do not replace the romantic experience with only boundary-setting lessons.
- Author-facing constraints belong in implementation and pre-play information. Avoid dialogue such as “you are also an adult”, API explanations, policy language, or a tutorial about consent inside a character's opening monologue. All romance characters remain adults, as required by the Seed.
- Keep scenes grounded in earlier player details. Use the original sound-archive premise: initially charming recordings gradually become altered versions of conversations, and the player must decide what to preserve, challenge, or leave behind. The game must not claim to inspect the real desktop or real private data.
- Creative chapter/mission elaborations and example lines are in `.ralph/director-notes.json`. They are optional source material consistent with the Seed, not evidence that implementation is complete.
- Original artwork supplied by the parent is in `public/assets`: both four-column expression sheets plus office, cafe, riverside, and archive PNG backgrounds. Generation prompts are in the two asset manifests. Preserve originals and integrate all suitable scenes.
- A private, Git-ignored `.env` has been provisioned locally with the user-authorized OpenAI credential. Never print it, copy its value to prompts, expose it to the client, or commit it. Model-list authentication was verified; no paid dialogue smoke calls were performed by the parent. Respect the Seed's total paid smoke-call limit.
- `docs/api-smoke-ledger.json` records the verified cumulative paid-response attempt count. The user has explicitly authorized this key's use. Reserve each actual smoke attempt in that ledger before calling, count failures too, and keep the overall limit of eight. Do not substitute mock success for live verification.
- Record real verification and remaining gaps honestly. Initial shell tests are not proof of completed missions, meaningful chatbot conversation, a full romance, or commercial readiness.
