# Direct original-art review

The parent opened all six current PNG sheets with `view_image`, then opened all six opaque Chromium composites listed in `sprite-alpha-index.json`. Each sheet contains four columns, so all 24 illustration cells were visually reviewed at sheet scale. These were actual supplied files, not newly generated assets or descriptive substitutes. No image was edited. The first transparent-image display showed coloured/fringed areas outside the silhouettes; the subsequent opaque browser composites did not show those areas. That initial tool presentation is not treated as a reproduced game rendering defect.

|---|---|
| boss-expressions | Clipboard held against torso; crossed arms and stern brows; raised hand adjusting glasses with surprised mouth; open palm and closed-eye smile. |
| boss-actions-a | Relaxed crossed arms/listening; hand on hip with smile; finger pointing at wristwatch; a sheet held out over the other open palm. |

| yuna-actions-b | Raised index finger and teasing smile; recorder held against chest; leaning forward while gripping cardigan; cassette extended while the other hand touches the chest. |

Identity, hair, accessories and outfits remain consistent enough to recognize each character across the sheets. Expressions, arm silhouettes and props provide distinct visible differences; catalogue aliases are not additional art. Boss actions-a has more canvas margin and smaller original figures; the current renderer already contains a sheet-specific transform. These composites do not verify every in-game pose transition or physical mobile input. The sheet-scale review is also not an independent human art rating, pixel-perfect inspection of every finger, or evidence of natural live-model pose selection. Existing current gameplay captures remain necessary for layout/occlusion judgments.
